## 💫 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer at [Synup](https://synup.com)** working full-stack across **FastAPI services**, **React frontends**, and the **CI/CD pipelines** that ship them. My focus is building software that holds up under real production traffic — APIs with proper authentication, rate-limiting, and observability; frontends with sane state management; Dockerized services on Kubernetes; and clean deployment pipelines on AWS.

Outside Synup I ship side projects across AI, blockchain, and e-commerce — including a **multi-model AI SaaS** ([Promptive AI](https://github.com/faizvk/promptive-ai)), an **AI-powered job-application platform** ([JobPilot](https://github.com/faizvk/job-pilot)), and a **Solidity-based e-tendering platform** I led as final-year team lead ([SmartChain Tender](https://github.com/faizvk/blockchain-based-contract-system)).

I value **clarity, correctness, and long-term maintainability** in code, and I'm continuously deepening both my systems fluency and my professional engineering practice.
